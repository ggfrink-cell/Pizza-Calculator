Recipe Notes

  1. Combine flour, yeast, and sugar in stand mixer or by hand.
  2. Add water on low until incorporated. Add ice to chill the water, remove the ice, and weigh the needed amount.
  3. Add salt and mix until combined.
  4. Add oil and mix until smooth.
  5. Perform stretch and folds until the dough passes the windowpane test.
  6. Bulk ferment.
    1. Type 1
      1. Ferment at room temperature for 12-18 hours.
      2. Portion dough into individual oiled containers.
      3. Let rest at room temperature for 1-5 hours before using.
    2. Type 2
      1. Ferment at room temperature for 12 hours.
      2. Refrigerate for at least 12 additional hours, up to 48 hours.
      3. Portion dough into individual oiled containers.
      4. Let rest at room temperature for 4-5 hours before using.
